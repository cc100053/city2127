# Close-up polish

Golden-sample workflow, proven on SE (2026-11-01; [handoff](../../../docs/handoffs/se-closeup-polish.md), [spec rounds](../../../artifacts/se-closeup-polish/spec.md)). The deliverable is the whole causal chain at one site: the guest's option words → before → change → after, read in two seconds from the close-up.

Roles: **Opus** (you) diagnoses, writes rubrics and specs, decides. **Sonnet** subagent (`model: sonnet`, `effort: high`) implements and captures. **Judge**: a fresh subagent every round, no history, scores rounds against the rubric. **Owner** alone accepts. Keep one Sonnet agent for the whole task and resume it each round, so it keeps context.

Lesson from NW (2026-11-03): R1–R3 went to the owner as "matches the target" while the bays had no visible people, no plaza, half the target's rings and glazing facing away from the camera. Opus judged silhouette and frame share only, graded Sonnet against its own spec (which already fell short of the target), sent known "weak" points as a pass, and reviewed 800×450 thumbnails. Steps 4–7 close each of those; [long-polish-loop](../long-polish-loop/SKILL.md) learned the same on 2026-11-02.

## Steps

1. **Preflight.** AGENTS.md startup, task branch `feat/<site>-closeup-polish`, handoff from `docs/handoffs/TEMPLATE.md`. Done: branch pushed with a PLANNED handoff.
2. **Diagnose.** Read the site builder in `src/siteBuilders/`, its `changeSites` entry (local units × `scale` = metres), its `SHOTS` poses and the option text in `docs/RESIDENT_COPY.md`. Capture −1/0/+1 at 2–4 candidate poses. Pick one pose framing all three states with sky or sea behind the subject. Done: one diagnosis table (cause per symptom) and a chosen pose in the handoff.
3. **Target images.** Save `baseline/` captures in the evidence folder `$CITY2127_EVIDENCE_ROOT/<site>-closeup-polish/` (default `~/city2127-evidence`) at the chosen pose; write `prompts.md`: one shared block (same camera, light, neighbours, in-engine low-poly style, Meter colour as the only accent) plus one block per state that restates the option text as form. A Sonnet agent generates them in Gemini through Claude in Chrome (see **Gemini target images**); the owner may instead run the prompts in any image-edit tool. Candidates go to `gen/`; the owner picks and winners are published into `artifacts/<site>-closeup-polish/target/` with `node scripts/publish-evidence.mjs`, never chosen by the agent. Done: owner's images saved in `target/`.
4. **Rubric.** Before any spec, write `artifacts/<site>-closeup-polish/rubric.md` per state (and per carrier, e.g. flagship and bay): 4–6 items, each an observable a judge can score from images alone, tied to a named crop rectangle (normalised x0,y0,x1,y1 of the review frame). Gemini enlarges and shifts the subject (NW flagship and bays, 2026-11-03), so measure the subject on the target and on the baseline capture: when they differ, write a second `capture [x0,y0,x1,y1]` rectangle framing the same subject where the city actually puts it, and ask the owner whether the target size is the goal (grow the model) or the crops follow the city (bay decision: 「Align crops, keep size」). Crop the baseline with [`scripts/crops.py`](scripts/crops.py) `prep` and check every crop shows its subject before any judging. Count what the target shows, e.g. "camera-facing side glazed, ≥ 6 people visible inside", "≥ 12 rings, spaced closer than ring thickness × 3", "paved forecourt with amber edge, benches and small trees". Size or frame share is at most one item. Done: every visible difference between baseline and target is an item, and every baseline crop frames its subject.
5. **Spec.** Write `spec.md` per state: parts with sizes in local units, materials (name each new one), Meter accent, life (people, furniture, planting), the transition read, camera, tests to update, screenshot list. From round 2 a spec section covers only the items below 2 and lists the 2-score items under **Keep**, with "re-crop and compare with the previous round". Name each Keep item's code (constants, functions, materials) in the spec: those regions are **locked**, and before capture you diff them against the kept round and revert any change the spec did not name. When one part feeds two items (a pavilion that is both `fl-pavilions` and the walkway it hides, an interior colour shared by every warm-room item), the spec names every item the part touches; change it only for the lowest item and list the others under Keep. Check every new colour or material in the spec against every rubric item (NW Bay R3: brown counters read as the beige mass `bl-material` forbids, and the round was lost). Check every rubric item against the spec: a spec line below the target (fewer rings, no forecourt, glazing facing away from the camera) is a **ceiling**, allowed only when listed in a `## Below target` section with the constraint that forces it (route clearance, budget, real metres) and approved by the owner. A constraint that blocks the target mid-round is the same: stop and ask the owner with options, never lower the design quietly. Done: every part in the target has a line, every rubric item is covered or owner-approved below target.
6. **Round.** Brief the Sonnet agent with only the files to read and the round's section, plus: move or split code only where the spec needs it, and at most three capture-compare iterations before reporting (NW Bay R1 took 41 min and 340k tokens on a refactor); it implements, passes `npm test && npm run build && git diff --check`, captures at 3200×1800 day and night at the review pose, names captures `<carrier>-<state>.png` and builds `vs-target.jpg`; you run `scripts/crops.py prep <rubric> <target dir> <rN> [<r(N-1)>] [--only <id prefix>]`, which writes `rN/crops/`, the blind `rN/judge/` and `rN/pair/` folders and `rN/pair-key.json`; only `vs-target.jpg` is published to `artifacts/`. Rerun the checks yourself; commit, push. Done: crops complete.
7. **Judge and decide.** Spawn a new judge subagent on `rN/judge/` only (rectangle-free rubric + target | round crops) — no reports, specs or round numbers; spawn the pairwise judge on `rN/pair/` in the same message. It scores 0 (absent) / 1 (present, off target) / 2 (matches the bar) with one line of evidence into `rN/scores.md`. A single absolute score moves ±1 on unchanged parts (NW R7: three untouched items fell 2 → 1), so from round 2 also run a second fresh judge on blind triptychs (target | previous round | this round, the two rounds in shuffled order, key kept out of its sight) that says per item which round is closer or `=`; `scripts/crops.py decode rN` writes `rN/pair.md` (score, decoded verdict, keep or not) and `rN/review.jpg`, the board you send the owner. Decide **per item**, not per round: the next kept state takes this round's code for items it wins or ties and restores the previous round's code for items it loses, wherever that code is separable (SW R7, 2026-11-05: R6's winners on R5 won 6–1); a whole round is kept or dropped only when its changes cannot be split, and then only when it wins more items than it loses. Judge the composed state again before the next spec. An item scored 2 counts as passed only when the pairwise verdict for it is not a loss. For the reported total use the median of three fresh absolute judges — one judge's total is noise (SE 2026-11-05: 21 → 18 → 20 on near-identical rounds); trends and keep decisions come from pairwise only. Any item below 2 → next round, spec aimed at the lowest items; a known weakness is a score below 2, never a note on a pass. Round 1 is calibration: show the owner the scores beside the crops, record their pass/fail words in `rubric.md` as the bar. After 3 rounds without all 2s, stop and report the score table. A round that is not kept: save `git diff` to the evidence folder, restore the source to the kept round, and record it in the handoff. Add an `## RN` handoff entry with the score row. Send the owner full-size crops (target | round per item, scores printed) and every item below 2 stated plainly. Done: all items 2 and the owner says accept, or the owner names the next round.
8. **Finish.** FPS: [`scripts/fps.sh`](scripts/fps.sh) `<vite url> <socket>:high` once — headed Google Chrome, hero day and night, the heaviest scene (owner 2026-11-04); discard the first sample, and measure main the same way only when the branch is below the last recorded hero figure. Headless numbers drift with machine load (two NW branch runs differed by 3 FPS) and settle nothing. Sync `PROJECT`, `EXHIBITION_SPEC`, `VALIDATION` and the handoff index; merge `--no-ff` per CONTRIBUTING in a temporary worktree of `origin/main` (symlink `node_modules`, run the checks, push `HEAD:main`, remove the worktree) when the checkout is shared with other sessions, and confirm main CI.

## Gemini target images

Proven 2026-11-01 (SE −1, ~3 min per image; owner-accepted as a pipeline). Needs Claude in Chrome connected (`list_connected_browsers` non-empty) and the owner signed in to Gemini; the agent never types credentials and stops on sign-in, CAPTCHA, consent or quota walls.

- Use `https://gemini.google.com/u/3/app` (owner's account index 3), new chat, tool **Create image**. `/app` is a different account.
- Brief a Sonnet agent (`model: sonnet`) with: the baseline PNG path, the shared block + the state block from `prompts.md` as one prompt, and the output path `gen/<state>.jpg` in the evidence folder. One image per state; resume the same agent.
- Upload: press `+` first so the hidden `input[type=file]` appears, `find` it, then `file_upload`. Paste the prompt with `javascript_tool` `document.execCommand('insertText')` into the editor.
- Download: hover the image, press the download button (full size, lands in `~/Downloads` as JPEG); keep the JPEG. The in-chat blob is only 1024 px.
- Output size is Gemini's choice (2752×1536 vs 1600×900 baseline): scale before side-by-side. Gemini garbles HUD/UI text in the corners; crop or ignore it, targets are for the site only.
- Judge each image: same camera and neighbours, in-engine low-poly look (not photoreal), orange lot outline kept, Meter accent only. Regenerate with a tightened prompt rather than editing the image.
- Fragile: UI changes break the clicks; automating the web UI may breach the provider's terms, so it stays opt-in per task.

## Lessons from SE

- `glass` mirrors the sky blue, so a glazed building reads like its blue neighbours. Camera-facing floors use `cream` slabs, `trim` mullions and an emissive warm backing.
- `publicLight` × instance colour turns white at night. Give the Meter accent its own emissive material.
- A high oblique camera flattens height. Close-ups sit low (15–25° pitch), facades over roofs.
- All states share one camera through a change. When the low state is small, use `<socket>:focal-low` (`CarrierChange.low`, `headlineShot`) and keep the other state of that change in frame.
- Growing a lot can drop surveyed trees; `plantCanopy` keys crown rotation by filtered index, so every later crown shifts and far clearance tests in `tests/odaiba.test.ts` fail. Shift the lot centre to keep those trees, and leave `coastalCanopy.ts` as is.
- Capture with `playwright-cli -s=<name>`: `resize`, `goto`, sleep ~10 s, `screenshot --filename=<abs>`, retry on the 5 s timeout. The in-app browser pane cannot screenshot while hidden.
- A Sonnet round took 11–22 minutes and 200–350k tokens; a precise spec is what keeps rounds few.
