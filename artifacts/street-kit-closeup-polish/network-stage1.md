`{centre: 0, half: 3.5, lane: 1.75}` and lanes sit at ±1.75 m (Japan keeps left; lane 0 with U, lane 1 against it). Length 1108 m.

## 3. GLB streets inside `DISTRICT`

`repairRoads` keeps its audit cuts and gains the stage-1 cuts: road, paving, markings, guardrails, street lights and public lighting
under the U's footprint (± its verges), plus the pieces it leaves behind: ② west of the corner and the old link, ①'s south-west extension
to its dead end, and the junction fill (`ROAD_FILL` / `roadFill` are deleted). Cuts are rectangles every 10 m along the U.
`ROAD_LINKS` keep the six-way junction patch; audit 9's link on ①'s extension goes with it. Kept until stage 2: the ④ band (`ROAD_MAJOR`, its markings and paving), its side streets, and the short connector
between ① and ② at the district's east edge. Beyond the district nothing changes.

The kit's meshes join the environment group (like the junction fill did), with the ceramic and footway materials named `road` and
`sidewalk`, so ground checks, doorway walks and the car-clearance test see the new street as street and its planters as solids.

## 4. Street cars, crossings, stop lines (production, no DEV override)

Everything reads one definition in `src/layout.ts` (`STREET_KIT`: U parts, reaches, crossings, gaps), which `streetKit.ts` builds and
`mobility.ts` drives on. `useKitCarriageway` and the `?roadKit` A preview go; `?roadKit` keeps only the B boulevard preview for stage 2.

- `CARRIAGEWAY = [{centre: 0, half: 3.5, lane: 1.75}]`, `ROAD_HALF = [3.5]`; one road, so one pair of lanes ≈ 43 cars each (now 2 × 2 × 19).
- `CROSSINGS`: U d 398 on ② (the accepted R3 avenue frame) and U d 545 on ③ (from the deck stair's foot to the west lawn). The kit draws
  the light-panel crossings at the same `d`s; mobility's own carriageway seams, lane guides and crossing bands (hidden under the kit
  in R3) are removed; crossers, stop lines (4 m before) and braking use `CROSSINGS` as now.
- `DROP_OFF` (as built) moves to ①'s footway along the plaza deck's north face, U d 750 and 700 (lane-1 metres 358.3 and 408.3 from U's
  end): that frontage has no planters, so the bays need no gap and the R3 avenue frame keeps its planters on both sides.
- `districtMeters` nw street pulse keeps its place (① d 408 → U d ≈ 1006).
