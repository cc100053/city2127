## Shared block
Edit the attached concept vehicle into a target image for a real-time 3D city set in Tokyo Odaiba in 2127. Keep the same camera angle, the same smooth egg-shaped teardrop silhouette, the wraparound glass canopy and the round disc at the tail. Change the following. Replace the dark studio and holographic platform with a pale paved city street in bright midday sun, soft shadow, a little lush green planting blurred behind. Body: clean matte-satin white metal in large simple forms, one pale grey lower panel instead of the black one. Remove every vent, grille, small slot and all surface clutter. Lights: one single continuous thin light seam around the body in low-saturation cool white-blue; remove all orange and all saturated cyan. Glass: clear and readable, so you can see inside. No text, no logos. Style: stylised in-engine real-time 3D render, low-poly, large clean forms with crisp bevels, not photoreal, not iridescent. Proportions of a compact 4.6 m long, 1.85 m wide city car.

Inside the clear canopy two passengers sit facing each other on pale seats; there is no steering wheel and no driver.

## car (human-driven)
This one is a human-driven car of the same design family. It sits on the road on four slim white disc wheels with thin dark tyres, no hover ring. It has a short distinct front hood and a raked windscreen, and inside one driver sits facing forward holding a small steering yoke, with one passenger beside them. The canopy is smaller and sits further back than on a driverless pod.
