# Car family rubric

Give one score per item, not per row. The board has three rows; left is the target, right is the in-engine round. Row 1 pod day, row 2 human car day, row 3 pod night. Score 0 absent, 1 present but off target, 2 matches. Camera, street, background and render realism differ by design and are not scored.

- `egg` all rows: egg or teardrop body, tallest behind the middle, nose low and tapered.
- `tail` all rows: round bulbous tail with a round disc on it.
- `seam` all rows: one continuous thin cool white-blue light seam that drops from the shoulder, runs low, steps up and goes to the nose. No orange, no saturated cyan.
- `glass` rows 1 and 3: large clear canopy over the front two thirds, a separate foot window below it, readable side window edge.
- `people` all rows: two occupants visible through the glass, seats readable as seats.
- `hover` rows 1 and 3: no wheels, thin emitter ring under the belly, light pool on the road.
- `wheels` row 2: four slim disc wheels with dark tyres in arches, grounded, smaller canopy, opaque roof spine.
- `finish` rows 1 and 2: pale satin metal with visible form shading, not blown out.
- `night` row 3: bright seam, warm-white nose band, red tail ring, warm interior.
