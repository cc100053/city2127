## Shared block
Edit the attached in-engine screenshot into a target image for a real-time 3D city set in Tokyo Odaiba in 2127. Keep exactly the same camera, the same buildings, trees, street and small cars below. Redesign the six-car white train on the elevated guideway into transit that 2027 could not build, so a visitor thinks "there must be technology here we have not invented yet". The impossible part must show its machine: precise seams, stabiliser rings, emitters and a visible field edge, never magic. Colours: white and pale satin metal, clear glass, technology light in low-saturation cool white-blue; no orange, no saturated cyan, no logos, no text. Style: stylised in-engine real-time 3D render, low-poly, large clean bold forms with precise glowing detail bands, not photoreal. Make the train dramatic and large in the frame.

## A — floating field train (day)
The train floats about 3 m clear above the guideway beam with nothing touching it, carried by a chain of thin glowing white-blue stabiliser rings that stand around the beam every few metres and light up as the train passes, and a translucent pale-blue field membrane under the cars between train and beam. The cars are long seamless streamlined capsules, the lead car's nose a long sharp sculpted blade of white metal sweeping to a point, the whole train one continuous body with flexible glowing joints instead of gaps. The upper half of every car is one continuous clear glass canopy showing passengers standing and sitting inside. Bright midday sun.

## B — ring-carried capsule train (day)
The guideway beam is replaced at the train by a floating line of huge white orbital rings (each about 8 m across) hovering in a row above the old beam; the train runs through the centre of the rings without touching them, held by a glowing white-blue field that links ring and train. The cars are fat glass-and-white-metal capsules with rounded bulbous ends, glass from top to belly so passengers are clearly visible, linked by short glowing field couplers with a visible gap between capsules. The lead capsule has a wide panoramic glass dome nose. Bright midday sun.
