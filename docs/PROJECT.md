## Scene, geometry and rendering

Coordinates are **metres, Y-up**. `DISTRICT` covers the 720 × 680 m hero area. The derived `odaiba_district_v01_environment.glb` retains connected ground/roads and quieter background context outside the hero; the former 150 m dissolve-to-island is superseded. Telecom Center is not loaded as a separate landmark. [crop script](../scripts/crop-odaiba-district.py) produces the environment from the preserved masterplan source.

Six named models remain: Aqua City, DECKS, DiverCity Plaza, DiverCity Office Tower, Hilton and Grand Nikko. Fuji TV's runtime form is `civicCore`. Generated maritime sky/coastal grass textures are local assets. The future-tree GLB is used by the Park site; it is no longer placed at Hachiko.

Fuji TV target v3 (2026-10-07, `codex/fuji-tv-polish`, round 8's three passes complete, pending visual review): two occupied work wings contain editing mezzanines, workstation/staff details against dark umber back walls under warm ceiling coves, sparse hung blue screens, narrower rear sealed studio volumes flanked by glazed side studio galleries, selective near-clear glazing and blue live-studio sets on three studio levels (Y=43/83/102); other gallery levels stay warm. The sphere has a taller broadcast wall with recessed centre scenery, warm proscenium, presenter desk/cameras, dark acoustic seating treads, tiered audience and technical grid behind reduced glazing ribs; a single inward stage-wash shell glows violet around the rig and fades to navy below the clear crown. Diagonal transfers are escalators with dark treads, a blue glass bay-side balustrade/fascia and blue-lit rails; the flank diagonal braces and pier light squares are removed. Warm room lighting and cool production screens follow the existing city clock through a shared shader uniform reading `cityRig.publicLight.emissiveIntensity`; building-local ceramic/furniture finishes leave shared city materials unchanged. Glazed public foyer, forum visitors/benches, bay-facing groups, mid-level promenade pairs and separate rear staff entrances express the approved program; people/equipment/access details are static geometry, without interior-service or access-control simulation. Promenade pairs stand at Y=65 on the transfer beam above the thinner slab. Split piers, planted diagonals, forum bridge opening, sphere berth, district routes, original Blender/GLB and authored hero remain. Static geometry merges into nine shared material batches. [Target, checks and limits](handoffs/fuji-tv-polish.md).

Hero camera: position `(-520,300,-620)`, target `(-40,25,-60)`, FOV 42°, near 2, far 9000. Orbit distance 150–1000, polar angle .35–1.42; pan is bounded to the district. Comparisons use the untouched hero pose, stated viewport/hour and actor time. No automatic camera reframing.

Renderer: WebGL 2, Neutral tone mapping (.84 base exposure), sRGB output, pixel ratio capped at 1.5, VSM directional shadows. The sky dome follows the camera. A softened generated sky plus RoomEnvironment light cards feeds PMREM reflections. Composer: 4× MSAA HalfFloat target → RenderPass → GTAO → bloom → vignette → OutputPass → display-space colour grade. Curved sea/bay context, wakes and Meter pulses are excluded where required from GTAO's flat override prepass. Do not add another tone-map/output conversion. Environment ground finishes lie within 0–3 cm of each other (building pads over lawn terrain, plazas, service yards), below hero-range depth precision; `odaibaScene` gives road/service, plaza, sidewalk and road-marking finishes increasing polygon offsets so they stack in that order over the lawn instead of z-fighting (2026-10-06).

Static groups are merged by material, windows/actors/carriers use InstancedMesh, and inactive district batches issue no draws. Shared resources are not disposed by a clone. Hidden transition transforms remain invertible. No geometry/material allocation per frame. Full teardown for repeated in-page scene recreation is not implemented; initialization is once per page.

## Frame and data flow

`main` determines clock/display hour → standalone `moodAt` or survey `world.state`, followed by `withNight`, supplies base atmosphere → lights/sky/fog update → `CityChangeManager.update(time,darkness)` advances focal sites, district carriers, pulses and local motion → `cityRig.update` forwards eased automation activity to mobility → `updateOdaiba` updates glazing → overlay and composer render. Time uses `performance.now()`; hidden tabs may advance motion on return.

A city day is 180 s from 12:00. Mood labels are Still / Daylight / Pulse; the old preset buttons and `0/1/2` controls were removed. `?hour=0..23.999` holds review time and overrides Admin Day/Night/Auto. Admin display setting persists independently of city reset. Separate Auto viewers have local clocks.

Standalone uses day-cycle activity and does not create the v2 district layer. `?survey` connects to `ws://<current-host>:8787/ws`, or the explicit ws/wss URL supplied as its value. Legacy v1 retains the 10 s atmosphere path. V2 uses the server layout and seeds directly, without blending scores into global atmosphere.

The district layer uses bounded preallocated slots: 17 climate bays, 11 service bays, six water rooms, ten courts, ten towers and 26 low pods, plus published roof carriers. Complementary covers preserve complete future identities; mixed composes hybrids. Cross-Meter agreement enables extra facilities. Four uint32 `slotSeeds` hash the full ordered active run history; neutral votes do not shuffle direction, and Undo excludes revoked proposals. Full-history hashing is O(guests) per view; optimize only if measured cost warrants it. Site/design footprints stay fixed while selected slot distribution can differ by history.

Automation changes walkers 400/260/120, pods 12/18/24 and circulating aircraft 2/16/30 at low/mixed/high, and turns street cars into autonomous street pods (all cars / about half / all pods, with the same amount of traffic); capacities exceed visible counts. Air tiers ([layout](../src/layout.ts)): the regional district loop/sphere approach at 160–170 m, the city shore lane (closed loop offshore, inbound leg 80 m, outbound 100 m) and the service quadrotors at 30–45 m. Aircraft slots 0–9 alternate loops evenly; slots 10+ fly as wingmen 34 m behind, so platoons appear only as automation rises. Each slot is a human-led winged craft or an autonomous pod; craft switch midway between settled levels (pods about 0/15/50/85/100 % for ports 0–1/2/3/4/5–6), berth shuttles become pods above mixed. Corridors keep a faint fixed guide line; four shore-lane beacon masts (58 m) stand clear of rooms and boat routes. The shore interchange in front of Aqua City has a 56 m pier, boat head and a 38 m arrival mast with glass lift; its boat and air shuttle reuse the 40 s `dockMotion` cycle (offset 20 s) and pivot over 4 s when leaving, as does the sphere shuttle. Walkers move in parties (per six slots: one alone, a pair, a trio with a child); four of five parties walk the promenades, every fifth a mid-level/garden skyway deck. Each makes one-way trips at their own pace (0.9–1.45 m/s; a third of lone walkers jog, in the outer lane so they pass parties), followers loosely ahead or behind, fading in and out at the route ends as if entering a building, rests out of sight, and picks a fresh direction. Lanes are right of travel, so the two directions keep to their own sides; parties walk at most 0.6 m wide (a pair abreast; a trio's child beside the leader, the other adult behind; single file on decks), and `passingLanes` makes everyone sharing a path sidestep to pass or meet with 0.65 m between shoulders, queueing up to 2.5 m where a crowd leaves no room; whoever passes a fixed mover (a halted robot, its collector, a stopper) goes round the side with room. Feet follow ground actually covered (trip distance plus the solver's along offset), so queued people stop stepping, and a sidestep turns the body toward its actual path. Two in five non-joggers stop at a viewpoint facing the bay. On the promenades each such party has its own spot (`promenadeStops`): lone walkers veer out of the lane to a backless one-seat stool between benches and sit 70 s, parties line up at a free rail spot for 40 s, then rejoin their lane; they stop only on trips with the sea on their right, so they never cross the oncoming lane; leftover stoppers and deck walkers walk on. Up to 120 doorway walkers (scaled like the promenade walkers) step out from 0.6 m inside one ground-floor door (so they emerge from the facade rather than grow out of the paving), cross the paved forecourt 4–10 m out on a corner-rounded curve (keeping right and making way like promenade walkers; each door on one trip, trips 3 m apart, one robot per trip) and go in at the next: `publishDoorways` raycasts each loaded landmark's facades and forecourt (sidewalk/plaza only). Street cars (4.6 m human-driven cars, every seventh a 7 m van; head/tail lights at night; each slot becomes a rounded autonomous street pod or cargo pod by the aircraft's `podShare` thresholds as automation rises, standalone keeps cars) drive on the left on two avenues snapped to the environment's road surface ([layout](../src/layout.ts) `streets`: seaside avenue north of Aqua City/DECKS, avenue under the guideway), one speed per lane with irregular non-closing gaps, fading at the avenue ends; density follows `traffic`. They do not cross, so there are no signals (2127 cars need none). Two cars on the guideway avenue's westbound lane make kerbside drop-offs at paved bays south of Aqua City: each eases into its bay, parks one full lane lap and rejoins its own slot, so it never meets another car (kerb-side amber indicators from 30 m before easing in, a brake lamp and slight nose dip while stopping, road-side indicators from 3 s before pulling away with a slight lift); a passenger walks between car and the nearest landmark door. The promenades are a 7.2 m paved boardwalk (top at walker level, lawn 0.2–0.35 m below). Benches every 24 m, 2.75 m out facing the sea (walker centres stay ≤ 2.1 m out, so shoulders clear the backrest; feet clear the lit edge), seat two people, with a couple standing at the rail between benches; up to 20 forecourts hold standing groups of two or three, and 14 delivery robots (0.7 m/s) run the doorway forecourts, halting 8 s just outside the next door while a collector steps out to meet them, then both go in. People swing legs and arms in a vertex shader (`gait` instance attribute, no extra draws; shadows and GTAO use the rest pose) and a quarter carry a backpack, a fifth a shoulder bag. `streetRhythm(hour)` thins each group through the day by a stable per-person hash: evening crowds, joggers at dawn/dusk, strollers toward sunset, children by day, sitters from late morning, robots at night, cars at the commutes; without an hour (tests, standalone callers) everyone stays. DEV `?review=street` frames a promenade bench and the seaside avenue from 40 m (orbit may close to 30 m in that mode only; `&cam=x,y,z` moves that camera). Sixteen transfers (independent of Meters) disembark/queue/board on the boat cycle. Every person wears a collar light lit at night. Guideway trains run one way on each of the two Yurikamome beams (`guidewayTracks`, traced from the environment: south-west on the centreline beam, north-east on the beam 4.6 m beside it, keeping left), evenly spaced at 12 m/s and fading beyond the district, so they never meet or reverse. Boats (`boatPoses`) have commensurate periods (loop taxis 20 s apart on a loop whose return leg runs 28 m seaward of the outbound, cruisers 40 s per run, interchange boat 40 s, ferry 320 s), and their phases were searched so 2.5 m of water stays around every hull over the 320 s period. Base water fleets, sweep cars and berth shuttle are independent. P12 adds up to 120 crowd slots for eligible courts (seed-zero 0/24/96), 11 service quadrotors with staggered 48 s docking cycles, and slow night accent rhythms. These are authored motion, not physics/pathfinding/occupancy simulation. Route/bounds checks are in [mobility tests](../tests/mobility.test.ts) and [Odaiba tests](../tests/odaiba.test.ts); detailed accepted limits in [Meter handoff](handoffs/archive/meter-variety.md).

Live targets ease over 3 s; changed slots emit faint Meter-colour rings and each changed Meter's site emits bright rings plus a light shaft (two waves, about 4.2 s; site-only shafts since 2026-10-04). Snapshot/reset/Undo/reconnect/reduced motion settle immediately and clear pulses/queued displays. No-op events do not replay. Late roof/asset publication must adopt the latest target and seed.

gesture, companions looking toward the speaker, then a quiet interval. A shared `social` instance attribute turns heads and raises
