## Scene, geometry and rendering

Coordinates are **metres, Y-up**. `DISTRICT` covers the 720 × 680 m hero area. The derived `odaiba_district_v01_environment.glb` retains connected ground/roads and quieter background context outside the hero; the former 150 m dissolve-to-island is superseded. Telecom Center is not loaded as a separate landmark. [crop script](../scripts/crop-odaiba-district.py) produces the environment from the preserved masterplan source.

Six named models remain: Aqua City, DECKS, DiverCity Plaza, DiverCity Office Tower, Hilton and Grand Nikko. Fuji TV's runtime form is `civicCore`. Generated maritime sky/coastal grass textures are local assets. The future-tree GLB is used by the Park site; it is no longer placed at Hachiko.

Fuji TV target v3 (2026-10-07, integrated from `codex/fuji-tv-polish` after round 9 by user request): two occupied work wings contain editing mezzanines, workstation/staff details on dark oak floors against dark umber back walls under softer warm ceiling coves, sparse deep-blue hung screens, dark front glazing mullions, narrower rear sealed studio volumes flanked by glazed side studio galleries, selective near-clear glazing and blue live-studio sets on three studio levels (Y=43/83/102); other gallery levels stay warm. The sphere has a taller broadcast wall with recessed centre scenery, warm proscenium, presenter desk/cameras, dark acoustic seating treads, tiered audience and technical grid behind reduced glazing ribs; a darkened inward stage-wash shell glows violet around the rig and fades to navy below the clear crown, with navy glass ribs, an outer ring of dome can lights, violet audience seats and a fully lit blue broadcast wall. Diagonal transfers are silver escalators with light treads, clear balustrades, a slim navy fascia and blue-lit rails; the flank diagonal braces and pier light squares are removed. Warm room lighting and cool production screens follow the existing city clock through a shared shader uniform reading `cityRig.publicLight.emissiveIntensity`; building-local ceramic/furniture finishes leave shared city materials unchanged. Glazed public foyer, forum visitors/benches, bay-facing groups, mid-level promenade pairs and separate rear staff entrances express the approved program; people/equipment/access details are static geometry, without interior-service or access-control simulation. Promenade pairs stand at Y=65 on the transfer beam above the thinner slab. Split piers, planted diagonals, forum bridge opening, sphere berth, district routes, original Blender/GLB and authored hero remain. Static geometry merges into nine shared material batches. [Target, checks and limits](handoffs/archive/fuji-tv-polish.md).

Hero camera: position `(-520,300,-620)`, target `(-40,25,-60)`, FOV 42°, near 2, far 9000. Orbit distance 150–1000, polar angle .35–1.42; pan is bounded to the district. Comparisons use the untouched hero pose, stated viewport/hour and actor time. Live v2 results with a headline now use `HeadlineCamera` (owner decision 2026-10-27): the 2.62 s vote journey stays at hero, then a 1.5 s smooth flight overlaps the existing 3 s site transition. Hold begins at 4.12 s, fly-out starts with the ambient card at 10 s and finishes at 12 s (actual `displayMs` preserves synchronization within the allowed 100 ms delivery jitter), before the 15 s lease. The reason appears at 3 s while the camera is flying. A newer result returns smoothly from its current pose to hero during its journey before approaching its own shot. Snapshot/reset/Undo/reconnect, reduced motion, legacy and catch-up results (`displayMs < 9900`) return immediately to hero. A fresh no-headline result stays at hero; when it interrupts an active shot it returns smoothly over 2 s during the new journey, reenables controls and has no fly-in. Standalone and other DEV review modes have no automatic headline playback.

The constant shot table has focal/district views for nw/ne/sw/se and garden/garden2/ring/wing/island. District wins when both focal and district change; result card and camera share energy priority waterGardens → receiver → flywheel → island, including removals. Update applies after OrbitControls and district pan clamp, preserving offshore targets without relaxing normal controls. DEV `?shot=nw:focal` / `window.cityShot(id)` pins a table pose (a standalone shot query also loads the neutral exhibition carriers); `cityShot()` prints/returns the current pose. `?meters=…&metersTo=…&metersAge=…` and `cityMeters()` share the live timeline without a server; other DEV comparisons retain their own camera.

Renderer: WebGL 2, Neutral tone mapping (.84 base exposure), sRGB output, pixel ratio capped at 1.5, VSM directional shadows. [renderQuality](../src/renderQuality.ts) reads `?quality=low` (2026-10-15, manual fallback for weaker GPUs, production too): pixel ratio cap 1, no MSAA, GTAO disabled and the shadow map off; the default keeps the full look and automatic quality stepping is not implemented. Since 2026-10-27 the default renders GTAO at half the canvas size (upsampled by its blend), uses 2× MSAA and redraws the shadow map 4 times per second on wall-clock time (the sun moves ~2°/s); `mobility.ts` fleets (people, vehicles, drones, boats, benches, guides) cast no shadows, while trees, buildings and Meter structures still do. The sky dome follows the camera. A softened generated sky plus RoomEnvironment light cards feeds PMREM reflections. Composer: 2× MSAA HalfFloat target → RenderPass → GTAO → bloom → vignette → OutputPass → display-space colour grade. Curved sea/bay context, wakes and Meter pulses are excluded where required from GTAO's flat override prepass. Do not add another tone-map/output conversion. Environment ground finishes lie within 0–3 cm of each other (building pads over lawn terrain, plazas, service yards), below hero-range depth precision; `odaibaScene` gives road/service, plaza, sidewalk and road-marking finishes increasing polygon offsets so they stack in that order over the lawn instead of z-fighting (2026-10-06).

Static groups are merged by material, windows/actors/carriers use InstancedMesh, and inactive district batches issue no draws. Shared resources are not disposed by a clone. Hidden transition transforms remain invertible. No geometry/material allocation per frame. Full teardown for repeated in-page scene recreation is not implemented; initialization is once per page.

## Frame and data flow

`main` determines clock/display hour → standalone `moodAt` or survey `world.state`, followed by `withNight`, supplies base atmosphere → lights/sky/fog update → `CityChangeManager.update(time,darkness)` advances focal sites, district carriers, pulses and local motion → `cityRig.update` forwards eased automation activity to mobility → `updateOdaiba` updates glazing → overlay and composer render. Time uses `performance.now()`; hidden tabs may advance motion on return.

A city day is 180 s from 12:00. Mood labels are Still / Daylight / Pulse; the old preset buttons and `0/1/2` controls were removed. `?hour=0..23.999` holds review time and overrides Admin Day/Night/Auto. Admin display setting persists independently of city reset. Separate Auto viewers have local clocks.

DEV-only `?energy=baseline|a|b|c|mixed`（配合`hour`／`reviewTime`）提供E3城市組合；只在沒有`survey`／`meters`／`scaleLab`時啟動。`startEnergyReview`回傳frame callback給現有`devFrame`，`energyPrototype.update(absoluteSeconds,reducedMotion)`同步兩水庭水位／管內流向、渦輪、富士前置環與兩處岸邊配電服務軌。60秒充能20／暫存20／釋能20；C配電獨立運作。36個合併／水面mesh與4個flow InstancedMesh一次建立，第二庭共用geometry／materials；只改可見性與transform／instance矩陣。系統reduced motion或原生「靜態閱讀」核取框固定本輪全部能源動態並保留箭頭／流程。Production移除DEV import／工具，正式四題及資料不變。[組合、座標、敘事／工程限制](ENERGY_CITY_PLAN.md#e3成果城市組合與慢速因果動態2026-10-24)，[實際桌面證據](../artifacts/energy-city-e3/README.md)。

Site/design footprints stay fixed while selected slot distribution can differ by history.

Air tiers ([layout](../src/layout.ts)): the regional district loop/sphere approach at 160–170 m, the city shore lane (closed loop offshore, inbound leg 80 m, outbound 100 m) and the service quadrotors at 30–45 m. Aircraft slots 0–9 alternate loops evenly; slots 10+ fly as wingmen 34 m behind, so platoons appear only as automation rises. Each slot is a human-led winged craft or an autonomous pod; craft switch midway between settled levels (pods about 0/15/50/85/100 % for automation levels 0–1/2/3/4/5–6 sixths; the live ladder only settles at 0, ½ and 1), berth shuttles become pods above mixed. Corridors keep a faint fixed guide line; four shore-lane beacon masts (58 m) stand clear of rooms and boat routes. The shore interchange in front of Aqua City has a 56 m pier, boat head and a 38 m arrival mast with glass lift; its boat and air shuttle reuse the 40 s `dockMotion` cycle (offset 20 s) and pivot over 4 s when leaving, as does the sphere shuttle. Walkers move in parties (per six slots: one alone, a pair, a trio with a child); four of five parties walk the promenades, every fifth a mid-level/garden skyway deck. Each makes one-way trips at their own pace (0.9–1.45 m/s; a third of lone walkers jog, in the outer lane so they pass parties), followers loosely ahead or behind, fading in and out at the route ends as if entering a building, rests out of sight, and picks a fresh direction. Lanes are right of travel, so the two directions keep to their own sides; parties walk at most 0.6 m wide (a pair abreast; a trio's child beside the leader, the other adult behind; single file on decks), and `passingLanes` makes everyone sharing a path sidestep to pass or meet with 0.65 m between shoulders, queueing up to 2.5 m where a crowd leaves no room; whoever passes a fixed mover (a halted robot, its collector, a stopper) goes round the side with room. Feet follow ground actually covered (trip distance plus the solver's along offset), so queued people stop stepping, and a sidestep turns the body toward its actual path. Two in five non-joggers stop at a viewpoint facing the bay. On the promenades each such party has its own spot (`promenadeStops`): lone walkers veer out of the lane to a backless one-seat stool between benches and sit 70 s, parties line up at a free rail spot for 40 s, then rejoin their lane; they stop only on trips with the sea on their right, so they never cross the oncoming lane; leftover stoppers and deck walkers walk on. Up to 120 doorway walkers (scaled like the promenade walkers) step out from 0.6 m inside one ground-floor door (so they emerge from the facade rather than grow out of the paving), cross the paved forecourt 4–10 m out on a corner-rounded curve (keeping right and making way like promenade walkers; each door on one trip, trips 3 m apart, one robot per trip) and go in at the next: `publishDoorways` raycasts each loaded landmark's facades and forecourt (sidewalk/plaza only). Street cars (4.6 m human-driven cars, every seventh a 7 m van; head/tail lights at night; each slot becomes a rounded autonomous street pod or cargo pod by the aircraft's `podShare` thresholds as automation rises, standalone keeps cars) drive on the left on two avenues snapped to the environment's road surface ([layout](../src/layout.ts) `streets`: seaside avenue north of Aqua City/DECKS, avenue under the guideway, which ends at d 620 short of Hilton's chapel and podium); `CARRIAGEWAY` places the lanes: the seaside lanes 0.25 m off centre away from Aqua City's wall, and under the guideway a carriageway widened 1.6 m left onto the paving slab (−5.1 … −0.5 m) so the Yurikamome piers, which stand in the right half of the surveyed road, stay ≥ 0.34 m clear of full-size cars, each never ahead of its slot on one speed per lane with irregular gaps, fading at the avenue ends; density follows `traffic`. Each slot is simulated frame to frame: it keeps 2 m + 0.5 s behind the car ahead round the loop, brakes for a requested crossing it can still stop for (≤ 3 m/s² profile, brake lamps lit while slowing or queued), then catches up (≤ +30 %, ≤ 14 m/s); first frame, jumps and snapshots put every car back on its slot. Two signal-free shared-space crossings on the guideway avenue (`CROSSINGS`, d 286 and 480; pale band, mint edge lines while requested): residents walk 8 m along the footway to the kerb, wait until every approaching car has stopped or passed, cross, and walk on along the far footway. The seaside avenue has none: Aqua City's wall stands 2.3 m from its centre line. Both carriageways are shared surface, not asphalt: inlaid darker stone seams (0.3 m) at the `CARRIAGEWAY` edges and a 0.1 m mint guide under each lane centre (`carriageway-seams`/`carriageway-guides`, one merged mesh each, flush on the paving slab under the crossing bands). `roadFill` lays a strip of the paving finish over the guideway avenue's junction mouth (d 486–507), which had only terrain. The two avenues do not cross each other, so there are no signals (2127 cars need none). Start → four answers → review → one proposal → result 10 s / handoff 5 s. Canonical request IDs and session uniqueness prevent double counting; transactions accumulate against latest state. A/B permits stale but not future city revision; single mode requires exact revision. Server schedules single/A-B displays ≥10 s apart and root preserves ordering/deadlines. `displayWaitMs`/`displayAt` are scheduling metadata, not viewer playback acknowledgement; `displayRemainingMs` resumes the original slot after retry/reload without extending it. Late expired events, disconnect/reconnect and reduced motion restore the latest snapshot and clear queued text/motion; lighting-only snapshots preserve valid queues/cards.

Single mode automatically hands off at next Start and applies a queued reset after the protected10s slot. A/B end also rejects release before its slot ends; early Guest handoff keeps recovery and defers station release. Explicit authoritative Undo/reset recovery remains immediate. A/B stops new admissions during reset and drains both; questionnaire expiry is 5 min, result lease fixed at display start +15 s (retry never renews it), server sweep every 1 s. Admin cancellation must identify one reserved session. Undo is available only for the latest completed proposal before a newer start; cancelling that newer draft does not reopen Undo. City/full reset and Undo retain original audit history; full reset changes the total-count watermark. Actual API/defaults are in [survey README](../survey/README.md).

## Performance diagnostics

They do not measure GPU allocation or per-frame latency.
