**Status:** Visual / world authority, rewritten 2026-11-02 by owner decision (cc100053). It replaces the earlier restraint-and-plausibility rulebook, now [history](../history/CITY_MASTER_TASTE_2026-11-02.md). Implementation rules: [ART](../ART.md); source map: [PROJECT](../PROJECT.md).

## North star

The guest's first-second reaction: **"There must be technology here we have not invented yet."**

Futurism comes from *visibly very advanced technology existing and working*, not from plausible urbanism. Impossibility is a means: a thing 2027 could not build tells the guest the technology exists.

## Principles

1. **Impossible, with proof of technology.** Every object beyond 2027 shows its machine signature: a floating mass has stabiliser rings, under-emitters and a field edge; a megastructure has precise seams, structural ribs and service rails; held water or light membranes have a field frame. Without a signature it reads as magic — not wanted.
2. **Skeleton: 3–5 megastructures carry the skyline.** One object is a district; people are ant-sized against it. The Sky Ring is the first of them and should gain presence.
3. **Focus: a few floating / anti-gravity pieces.** Two to four in the hero frame, each with a role (floating civic chamber, hovering garden, self-assembling building with modules joining mid-air).
4. **Technological finish over restraint.** At hero scale: white metal, precise lines, glowing seams, translucent field membranes, holograms. A glow must come from a system (mobility, energy, field, occupied rooms, Meter) — no random glowing objects.
5. **Daily life goes on.** People walk, trees grow, traffic flows. Residents treating the impossible as normal is what sells it.
6. **Designed for low poly.** Large clean forms plus precise detail bands (seams, light bands, emitters). No realistic window grids or rooftop-tree filler; close-ups should survive because forms, not textures, carry the read.

## Technology signature vocabulary

Use these words in target prompts and part specs: anti-gravity floating block, maglev / orbital ring, force-field dome or light membrane, energy column / light beam, self-assembling building, suspended water body / water wall, hologram layer, drone swarm formation, giant service arm.

## Colour

- **Technology light:** cool white-blue, low saturation — fields, seams, emitters, beams.
- **Meter colours** stay the only saturated accents (automation `#3fa9ff`, sharing `#ff6f91`, environment `#5fe08a`, concentration `#ffb347`).
- Base materials: white / pale metal, clear glass, living green, blue Tokyo Bay.

## Anchors kept

- Odaiba stays recognisable: Tokyo Bay, Rainbow Bridge, Fuji TV DNA (frame, sphere, diagonal) as lineage, not as limits.
- Everything else may move or be redesigned, including the Sky Ring, holograms and the four Meter facility forms; their roles in the exhibition flow stay.
- The hero does not have to show the four Meter sites: no sightline or visibility rule may block a megastructure or other design (owner 2026-11-02). Changes are shown by the headline close-ups and the vote journey.
- 60 FPS (vsync, 1920×1080) on the exhibition view; all UI text in Japanese.
- Greenery stays lush; futurism through form (edges, terraces, hovering beds), never sparse planting.
